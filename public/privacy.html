Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and username. When you submit a review, we store the review content, rating, and associated metadata.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide the Roamly service, display reviews, and manage your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Security

Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We use secure HTTP-only cookies for authentication. However, no system is completely secure.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is essential for the service to function and does not track you across other websites.

Your Rights

You can update or delete your account data at any time through your profile settings. You can delete your reviews individually.
