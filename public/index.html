Planning Your First Solo Trip in India

India offers a rich diversity of landscapes and cultures that make it an ideal destination for solo travellers. From the mountains of Himachal Pradesh to the backwaters of Kerala, here are some starting points:

  • Start with well-connected destinations like Jaipur or Goa
  • Research local transport options before arriving
  • Keep digital copies of important documents
  • Connect with local travel communities for recommendations

Best Seasons for Travel in India

India's climate varies dramatically by region. Understanding seasonal patterns helps you make the most of your visit:

  • October–March: North India, Rajasthan, and most popular destinations
  • September–May: South Indian hill stations and Kerala
  • June–September: Ladakh (post-snowmelt)
  • July–September: Monsoon season brings lush greenery to the Western Ghats
